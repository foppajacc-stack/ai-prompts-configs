# Grok Prompt Templates

Use these prompts with Grok (xAI) for real-time X/Twitter data, breaking news, and candid analysis.

---

## Real-Time X/Twitter Trends

```
What are people on X saying about [TOPIC] right now?
Summarize the main sentiments, top voices, and trending threads.
Note any viral posts or controversial takes.
```

---

## Breaking News Analysis

```
What is the latest breaking news on [EVENT/TOPIC]?
Cross-reference multiple sources and flag any conflicting reports.
Include the most recent updates as of now.
```

---

## Unfiltered Opinion Check

```
What does the public actually think about [TOPIC]?
Give me the raw, unfiltered range of opinions:
- Strong supporters and their arguments
- Strong critics and their arguments
- The moderate majority view
Don't sugarcoat; give the real picture.
```

---

## Stock and Crypto Sentiment

```
What is the current social media sentiment around [STOCK/CRYPTO SYMBOL]?
- What are traders and investors saying on X?
- Any notable influencer takes?
- Recent news catalysts driving discussion?
```

---

## Sports and Entertainment Live

```
What is happening with [TEAM/CELEBRITY/EVENT] right now?
Live reactions, scores, announcements, and fan sentiment on X.
```
