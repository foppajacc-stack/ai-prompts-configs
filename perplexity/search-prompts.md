# Perplexity Search Prompt Templates

Use these prompts in Perplexity AI to get better, more focused search results.

---

## Deep Research Mode

```
Search for the most recent and authoritative sources on [TOPIC]. 
Focus on peer-reviewed studies, official reports, and expert analysis. 
Summarize key findings and include publication dates.
```

---

## Comparison Research

```
Compare [OPTION A] vs [OPTION B] based on:
- Current pricing and plans
- Key features and limitations  
- User reviews from the past 12 months
- Expert recommendations
Cite sources for each claim.
```

---

## News and Current Events

```
What are the latest developments on [TOPIC] from the past week?
Focus on verified news sources only.
Provide a timeline of key events.
```

---

## Technical How-To

```
Provide step-by-step instructions for [TASK] using [TECHNOLOGY/TOOL].
Include:
- Prerequisites and requirements
- Common errors and fixes
- Official documentation links
Make sure the information is current (not outdated tutorials).
```

---

## Market and Business Research

```
Research the current state of [INDUSTRY/MARKET]:
- Market size and growth trends (cite latest reports)
- Key players and recent moves
- Emerging opportunities and risks
- Analyst predictions for next 1-2 years
```
